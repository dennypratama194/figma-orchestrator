# Design QA report

## Verdict

`pass`, `pass-with-notes`, or `fail`

## Blockers

Issues that prevent final approval or implementation.

## Findings

For each finding include severity, Figma page/frame/node, evidence, impact, and recommended correction.

## Coverage

- Required pages and states
- Responsive behavior
- Components and variables
- Content completeness
- Accessibility
- Interaction and prototype notes
- Implementation annotations

## Residual risks

Record unresolved assumptions and decisions that must survive handoff.
