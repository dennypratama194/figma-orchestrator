# Artifact contracts

Every artifact begins with project name, stage, status, date, upstream inputs, authoring specialist, and unresolved assumptions.

## Brief

Include business, offer, audience, primary action, success measure, scope, supplied evidence, brand inputs, constraints, missing information, assumptions, and contradictions. Separate facts from interpretations.

## Strategy

Include positioning, audience jobs and objections, message hierarchy, credibility plan, conversion strategy, differentiation, content risks, and measurable design implications.

## UX

Include sitemap or screen inventory, primary flows, page purpose, section anatomy, information hierarchy, interactions and states, mobile priorities, and rationale tied to strategy.

## Content

Include final or clearly labeled provisional copy by page and section, CTA hierarchy, proof requirements, metadata needs, and content gaps. Do not invent customer claims, metrics, awards, certifications, or testimonials.

## Direction

First produce `taste.md` using `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/templates/taste-profile.md` and the `apply-design-taste` skill. Then use `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/templates/direction.md`. Convert adjectives into concrete layout, type, color, imagery, composition, and motion rules. Cite dated reference traits; do not copy a reference's distinctive expression.

## Figma production records

List the file URL, page and frame names, node links or identifiers, dimensions, purpose, component coverage, unresolved issues, and what changed after critique. A claim that content exists must be verifiable in Figma.

## QA

Use `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/templates/qa-report.md`. Findings need severity, precise location, evidence, impact, and correction. A passing verdict cannot contain unresolved blockers.

## Handoff

Include approved frame inventory, foundations and variables, component map, responsive rules, states, content source of truth, asset inventory, interaction notes, implementation caveats, accepted risks, and the final approval record.
