# Anti-slop signals

These are diagnostic signals, not universal bans. A flagged choice may remain only when the designer can tie it to content, brand, user need, and the wider system.

## Structural signals

- interchangeable centered hero followed by logo strip, three cards, alternating image-text rows, testimonials, pricing, FAQ, and CTA;
- every section using the same width, alignment, spacing, and visual weight;
- a bento grid chosen before the information relationships are understood;
- repetitive dashboard screenshots inside identical floating cards;
- arbitrary section order with no narrative escalation;
- oversized empty space that creates length without tension or meaning;
- mobile layouts that merely stack desktop modules in source order.

## Surface signals

- decorative blue or purple glow used to manufacture perceived technology;
- generic aurora, mesh-gradient, blurry-orb, liquid-chrome, or animated-noise shaders without a concept-specific role;
- glassmorphism, gradients, pills, shadows, or rounded rectangles applied everywhere;
- tiny uppercase eyebrow labels above every heading;
- generic geometric sans typography with no intentional voice or hierarchy;
- random icons, abstract blobs, starbursts, grids, or noise textures unrelated to the brand;
- excessive micro-decoration that competes with content;
- fake product UI, fake logos, invented metrics, synthetic testimonials, or implausible imagery.

## Authorship signals

- direction variants differ only in color, font, or hero image;
- unrelated brands receive the same layout system;
- visual choices are described only with vague adjectives;
- references are imitated instead of decomposed into principles;
- every visible area is polished but no moment has a deliberate point of view;
- complexity is added because the design feels basic, rather than correcting the underlying concept.

## Correction sequence

1. Re-state the audience, message hierarchy, and single visual idea.
2. Remove treatments without evidence.
3. Rebuild page rhythm and attention before restyling modules.
4. Create one content-led signature moment.
5. Strengthen typography and imagery art direction.
6. Verify that the system remains coherent, responsive, accessible, and buildable.

A shader does not excuse weak composition. Remove it temporarily: if the hierarchy collapses or the design becomes interchangeable, fix the underlying direction.
